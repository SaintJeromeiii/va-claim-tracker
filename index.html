38 CFR § 3.310 — Secondary service connection

Legal basis for both claims

Disability proximately due to or the result of a service-connected disease or injury shall be service connected. You can win on causation and/or aggravation. Ask any nexus opinion to address both.

Migraines — § 4.124a DC 8100

%Criteria
50Very frequent completely prostrating and prolonged attacks productive of severe economic inadaptability
30Characteristic prostrating attacks averaging once a month over last several months
10Characteristic prostrating attacks averaging one in 2 months over last several months
0Less frequent attacks

Prostrating (DBQ): extreme exhaustion, powerlessness, debilitation, or incapacitation with substantial inability to engage in ordinary activities.

Urinary frequency / nocturia — § 4.115a

%Criteria
40Daytime voiding < 1 hour, or awakening to void 5+ times per night
20Daytime interval 1–2 hours, or awakening 3–4 times per night
10Daytime interval 2–3 hours, or awakening 2 times per night

Nexus theories to document

  • Migraines: PTSD stress/hyperarousal as trigger or aggravator; sleep loss; flares coinciding with PTSD; literature linking PTSD and migraine disability.
  • Nocturia: hyperarousal/nightmares waking you; fragmented sleep; PTSD medication side effects; next-day worsening of PTSD from sleep loss.

Official text: § 3.310, § 4.124a, § 4.115a.

C&P exam prep — your answers

Saved in this browser. Copy into Google Docs before the exam.

Day-of checklist

  • Upload latest logs to VA.gov before the appointment.
  • Bring printed migraine log, nocturia log, medication list, statements.
  • If symptom-free that day, say so — then describe a typical attack from the log.
  • Use counts, not “often.”
  • Describe interruptions of ordinary activity for prostrating attacks.
  • Ask (politely) that aggravation be considered if causation is doubted.
